Good to Know

Wedding questions, answered

  • Can The Governor's Inn host both the ceremony and the reception?

    Yes. Ceremonies happen in the gardens at the gazebo, and receptions run under the tented courtyard or in the ballroom, so the whole day stays on one estate. Call (603) 332-0107 to talk through the spaces.

  • What events does The Governor's Inn host besides weddings?

    Rehearsal dinners, birthday and anniversary parties, baby and bridal showers, celebrations of life, and business and fundraiser events, in the same rooms and gardens the weddings use.

  • Can our wedding guests stay overnight at the inn?

    Yes. Twenty original guest rooms sit right upstairs, each with a private bath, air conditioning, cable, free local calls, and high-speed wireless, with a continental-plus breakfast included. Book online or call (603) 332-0107.

  • How do we see the estate before booking?

    Call (603) 332-0107 to set up a tour, or email the events team with your event type, preferred date, and guest count.

  • Where is The Governor's Inn?

    78 Wakefield Street, Rochester, NH 03867, the historic Spaulding estate, two minutes off the Spaulding Turnpike. Look for the hanging Restaurant & Hotel sign out front.
